I WAS NEVER ENOUGH 😵‍💫

My life journey was always based on improvisation because I was poor and have ADHD.

I studied in almost a hundred different things.

Photography, video, editing, fashion production, aesthetics, acupuncture, self-defense, Integrative Therapies, Design, Classical Drums (yes, that exists 😂), music (about 25 instruments in total), HR, Human Development, meditation, Yoga, and so on.

But nothing was ever enough because I was always chasing something new.

I've always been like this. We creative people are like this.

Until one day I realized that many years later, this gave me a range of resources that no one around me had.

My personal complexity started to solve complexities that no one else could solve, at least not the way I do.

Then my weakness became my strength.

There's no point in sticking to an ideal learning model, because by the time we learn the game, the rules have already changed.

No knowledge is lost. None.

Be a complex person, be strange, don't make sense. That's good.

Being enough is not a seal you earn from a journey.

Being enough is always knowing that enough never arrives because everything changes all the time.

Think less and throw yourself more. I promise it's worth it.

PSYCHOANALYSIS AND LACK OF CHARACTER

In my psychotherapy training, I studied psychoanalysis quite a bit (which I have a love-hate relationship with) and there I learned a concept that literally changed my life.

Psychic economy.

Economy here is not presented in the sense of money but in the sense of organization and distribution of energy.

From a psychoanalytic perspective, each person with everything they have lived, thought, felt, learned, witnessed, etc., creates a UNIQUE model of thinking and living the world. Creates their own psychic economy.

Our social vision of character is very much tied to each person's level in society, that is, primarily education and money.

After all, they always suspect the maid will steal from the boss's house and will always doubt the corruption of a rich white man.

When we, born poor, ascend socially, we face the frustration of social dynamics between classes.

We realize that the "person" factor is not related to how much they studied or what they studied. Technical education does not prevent them from being racist, sexist, homophobic, etc.

You can have federal education, postgraduates, MBA, doctorate and still be a person of despicable character.

Always be suspicious.

THE FINANCIAL LOSS OF MOTHERHOOD

Who here has never heard this speech, right? Especially for those who heard it without a facial expression that could cause you problems, which was not my case.

But here I will use a simplistic example, but common in everyone's memory. Our beloved financial excesses of politicians.

When a woman gets pregnant, a plan is needed to temporarily replace her activities until she finishes her leave and can return.

And that has a cost, of course.

But do you know where we find costs?

Expensive hotels for leadership's travel. (and there's almost always a policy that the position you have defines the value of the room to be reserved).

Very expensive lunches paid for the core group.

End-of-year parties (which also usually have the one for the people and the closed one for the elite).

There are companies that as a monthly bonus package for high positions include a luxury car with all expenses and gasoline paid by the company.

Well, now look, we have expenses here and there, but do you perceive the difference in where the expense is worth it? Do you perceive that it is nothing more than disrespect for the maternal figure and appreciation for the pleasures of those who are valued?

Corporate elites still play this pretend game at the height of the digital age and think that it sticks with someone. It hasn't worked for a long time.

And it's their right to choose this, obviously.

But I start 2024 deeply wanting them to face head-on the consequences of their choices, and that mainly involves a lot of public exposure with faces stamped on it.

Even though some people think this post and my opinion a bit "exaggerated", as someone who has gone through so much internally and externally in many companies and cultures, I can confirm: this is nothing compared to the exaggeration of violence that women experience within companies.

So yes, I will intrinsically reinforce: the problem is not motherhood, nor expenses, nor logistics.

It's simply lack of character and a lot of incompetence.

FEEDBACK IS NOT ALWAYS USEFUL, YOU KNOW?!

Whether we like it or not, 90% of feedback is useless, empty, and devoid of minimal cognition that makes sense of what is being said.

And going even further down the path of honesty, I say: Feedback is a tool for maintaining social spaces.

And for us diverse people, usually these feedbacks cause ENORMOUS upheaval even though we know all the biases of whoever is speaking. It doesn't matter, it will still hurt for N reasons.

But it is a cognitive training to be done, especially on our perception of social class.

When a white man is promoted with higher education and 1.5 years of experience and a Black woman is promoted with a degree, 2 postgraduates and taking an MBA and 5 years of experience, where is the error?

And guess who will get the feedback that something is missing, that this year is your chance to learn more to move on to the next career and blah blah blah?!

Am I telling you to ignore your feedback? Basically yes!

Remember this image: is the person who criticizes you a success reference? Is it someone you admire? Is it someone who understands you your experiences?

If yes, throw yourself and learn as much as possible even if it's uncomfortable.

If not, ignore it. And without guilt!

You know why? Because the ruling class of this story has always done this without guilt. They've always sold themselves more than they are, always promised more than they delivered, always knew that the position of "positively arrogant" was good for business.

I'm not saying to be the same, because obviously the system doesn't allow it. But be a person who knows how to guide themselves and knows who can give you good things and who can give you immobility and depreciation through discourse.

And if you're scared of the results of that, it's always good to remember that it's more important for you to know how to plan and build a CAREER than just to live in repetition within a single job that paralyzes you.

Knowing how to measure is the way.

Knowing how to feel is already the trick.
