## Personal Journey & Philosophy

### From Adversity to Purpose
Paulo describes his journey as one of overcoming adversities, having grown up in the peripheries of Rio de Janeiro in a context of financial difficulties and domestic violence. This experience shaped his desire to "make this world less complicated" and to use his curiosity and intelligence to create resources and pathways for others facing similar challenges.

### The Power of Complex Knowledge
*"I was never enough. My life journey was always based on improvisation because I was poor and have ADHD. I studied in almost a hundred different things... But nothing was ever enough because I was always chasing something new. Until one day I realized that many years later, this gave me a range of resources that no one around me had. My personal complexity started to solve complexities that no one else could solve. Then my weakness became my strength."*

Paulo's unconventional path—spanning photography, music, design, HR, therapy, neuroscience, and activism—is not a liability but his greatest asset. This multidisciplinary foundation enables him to approach problems from angles others cannot see and to serve populations often overlooked by traditional practitioners.

### Professional Philosophy
- **No knowledge is lost.** Every learning experience contributes to a unique resource pool.
- **Being enough is not a destination.** It's an ongoing practice of self-acceptance amid constant change.
- **Think less and throw yourself more.** Action and experience are teachers no theory can replace.
- **Inhabit the in-between worlds.** Innovation happens at intersections, not within silos.
- **Complexity is strength.** Being "too much" or "too strange" is often exactly what the world needs.
