# Complete Profile: Paulo Rezende

This detailed profile was compiled from publicly available information on [DiverCidade Hub](https://www.divercidade.net/paulo-rezende), a professional resume document (PDF), and a presentation image. The objective is to provide a complete and integrated view of Paulo Rezende's professional, academic, and personal trajectory.

## Executive Summary

**Paulo Rezende** is a multifaceted professional and entrepreneur with a remarkable trajectory that moves between **Strategy and Marketing**, **Human Development**, and **Diversity & Inclusion (D&I)**. He combines solid training in neurosciences applied to human behavior with deep experience in communication, management, and social activism.

Currently, he holds key leadership positions:
*   **Founding Partner** at **ESGX** (since Sep/2024).
*   **CSMO (Chief Strategy and Marketing Officer)** at **DiverCidade Hub**.
*   **CEO** of **Uprise Creative Agency** (since Nov/2023).
*   **President Director** at **UmMaisUm (NGO)**.

His specialty lies in the intersection between **People and Management** and **Marketing**, focusing on how companies and individuals can communicate authentically and effectively, especially on diversity topics. He is an expert in neurosciences applied to human behaviors, a Sexologist and Psychotherapist, using this training to support people through the Center for Studies in Diversity and Sexuality (NEDS).

## Personal and Professional Trajectory

Paulo Rezende describes his journey as one of overcoming adversities, having grown up in the peripheries of Rio de Janeiro in a context of financial difficulties and domestic violence. This experience shaped his desire to "make this world less complicated" and to use his curiosity and intelligence to create resources and pathways.

He began his career in **Communication**, moved to the **People and Management** area, and finally specialized in **Applied Neurosciences** applied to human behavior. His approach is marked by the perception that the classical way of approaching topics like D&I and communication was outdated, which led him to "create" his own path, inhabiting the "in-between worlds" of communication, diversity, and neuroscience.

## Personal Insight

Paulo identifies himself as "PF Rezende, alias 'Ready Meal'" and, despite considering himself a "dramatic artist (one could say pessimist) by nature," he carries a strong desire for social impact. His life is proof that it is possible to create resources and success from a difficult beginning, using his story to influence the corporate world to be more **healthy, safe, and inclusive**. His orbit, despite apparently distant paths, has always been centered on **people**.

## Conclusion

The analysis of the data reveals a professional of **high performance** and **deep social awareness**, who uses his life experience and vast academic knowledge to serve as a transformation agent in large corporations and in the third sector. His career is a successful convergence of **business, neuroscience, and activism**, positioning him as an influential voice and strategic leader on D&I topics and human development in Brazil.
