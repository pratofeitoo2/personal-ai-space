This detailed profile focuses on Paulo Rezende's expertise in the areas of **Applied Neuroscience**, **Sexology**, and **Psychotherapy**, highlighting his training, clinical practice, and the application of this knowledge in contexts of human development and diversity.

## Professional Summary

**Paulo Rezende** is a specialist who integrates the knowledge of **Neuroscience Applied to Human Behavior** with the practice of **Sexology** and **Psychotherapy**. His approach is grounded in the scientific understanding of the mind and behavior, applied to personal development, sexual health, and emotional well-being, with a particular focus on diversity and inclusion issues.

He uses this training to serve as:
*   **Applied Neuroscience Specialist** in human behaviors.
*   **Sexologist** and **Psychotherapist**.
*   Supporter of people within the theme of diversity and inclusion through the **Center for Studies in Diversity and Sexuality (NEDS)**, part of DiverCidade.

## The Intersection of Neuroscience and Therapy

Paulo's specialty lies in understanding the **functioning of the human mind** and how it perceives the world, applying this knowledge to promote human development and effective communication. His training in Neuroscience and Human Development allows him to address issues of behavior and well-being from a scientific and integrated perspective.

He realized that the classical approach to addressing human development topics was outdated, which led him to inhabit the "in-between worlds" of communication, diversity, and **neuroscience applied to human development**.

## Clinical and Social Work

As a **Sexologist and Psychotherapist**, Paulo Rezende puts his training into practice through the **NEDS (Center for Studies in Diversity and Sexuality)**, where he supports people within the theme of diversity and inclusion.

His life experience, marked by overcoming adversities, reinforces his desire to use his expertise to promote a "less complicated" and more inclusive world. His work at NEDS and training in clinical care for the LGBTQIAPN+ population demonstrate a direct commitment to the mental and sexual health of underrepresented groups.
