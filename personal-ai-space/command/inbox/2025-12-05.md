## 📝 Notes & Thoughts

I keep having this #heaviness on my shoulders about making all this #effort and #wasting all this #time for no good reason. Or better put, for no reliable reason. 

No matter how much things fluctuate in my #marriage I can’t avoid the feeling that it’s all gone to shit, that is already too late. 

I can’t change my brain to go around this #thought and walk another path. And I keep trying to ignore many things until I got somehow used to it but I’m not capable. 

I feel that now we kinda landed, but we’re too far from each other. I can’t stop #feeling that she’s too #immature, to #dependent, too #needy. 

I have lived 10 years of #fatherhood before she became a mother. And that means a lot about how we feel and see things in different ways. 

We landed close enough to see each other but too far away to live in the same world. 

This life is breaking me, it’s breaking her and soon will break my boy. 

I keep living this life of mending every shit she’s done, always questioning myself how the fuck she did something so #stupid, so #reckless. 

And I’m the meantime I don’t live. She’d consumed all live I had and keep consuming every life I could get. And I’m #exhausted of #feeling this eternal gravitational pull of her drawing and dragging me in.
