## 📝 Notes & Thoughts

After i get back home knowing i've lost another trip to get the kids i was in a really trash mood, just wanted to be alone. 

Which of course became a issue after [[Tamara]]  not being able to let me alone. I was angry as fuck, and had too little in me to run the script we usually do. I couldnt bear the countless hours of her talking over and over again. So i lashed out pretty bad. 

And the looping looped. Things got pretty bad. 

She was crying all the time, i was drowning in anger and frustration, and the previous fight we had was coming out from the shadows and becoming quite alive.

I saw she doing exactly what she promised she wouldn't do. Again. 

This looping kept going all day long, until eventually we're both to tired to keep fighting, crying and arguing about anything. Somehow we find some seconds of peace among the exhaustion we're feeling. And slowly those seconds became minutes, and for the first time in weeks we had 30 minutes of peace. We laughed together, we came to some kind of unspoken agreement of peace. 

It wasnt magical, it wasn't a solution
