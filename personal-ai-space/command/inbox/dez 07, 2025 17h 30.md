Today my mother in law came to our house to enjoy the pool and as always i wasn't #feeling in the #mood to #socialize, so i interacted the minimum as possible, but at some point i joined them in the pool for like 15 minutes or so and on this moment i noticed a thing that have been around my mind for few times: we don't have what to talk about. 

Not exactly don't have, but don't match the talks. 

It's most likely me who sucks at #interaction when i'm in bad mood, but i don't think it ends on that. Specially about me and my wife. Because at every day we seem to have run out the #possibilities of what to talk about. Except #problems. And that's what got me #worried. 


There are a thousand reasons why we got this bad on talking and just chilling together, but i guess that in real life it doesn't matter if you have 1 reason or 1000 because they'll end up with us living the #problem itself. 

But i can't avoid thinking: what if this one is on me? 

And without judgement, because i have my many reasons to, but i can help to think that i'm really really #broken down to this point where sitting down in the couch and having a #conversation feels like a #nightmare to me?

This is one item of the list of reasons why i can't seem to #function well in society. Not anymore. 

Mostly because i'm quite #sad and #worried all the time, must also because how much #spiteful i got?

I do have #reasons to be #sad, to be #mad, to be #angry, to be whatever the fuck i be, but reason don't make the #problem go away. So with reasons or not, how this version of me fits in this #family of mine? Because i don't seem to have even a half answer to that.

I think that this happens because of a few #possibilities, but my top list choice is the fact that we can't see the world the same way, we can't see the #past, or the #future the same way, and that leaves us spinning and arguing on two different orbits that leads to nowhere. 

And as i said, im spiteful. 

I won't waste my time talking again about things that hurt me for no #reason. Because in the end we always reach the same #pattern. Everything she says she understand she proves that she did not, everything she says she've changed she prove she's not. 

And she changed a lot of her #behavior, but i can see inside her that the core problem still there. For me the root matters. #Behaviors can have many shapes and forms, can look different, better, worse, bigger, smaller, doesn't matter, but they will always be a #projection of the core. Shake the #projection enough and you will see the core exposed. 

She's trying indeed, she's hurting trying to always get better and better, but this is a path she should've taken many many years ago, and i have two options available, which is cross her path or leave her path. For years i crossed with her. Now i can't any longer. I left. I'm always at the margins, always on the sides leaving her to deal with whatever comes her way. I've been harmed enough. 

And that's where things fall back on our #interaction as a couple being so trash nowadays. There is always something i will spot from her. A phrase, a look, a change in tone of voice. And you remember when we were like this level of #hyperaware of others #behaviors? Exactly. 
We're fucking deep routed on #trauma. 

And when i've tried telling her that her core still there in shapes, colors and ways that she can't see the #feedback i got was that i'm always #labeling her as a #monster and that now she's so much different. 

This #conversation happens hundred of times and it left us wasted in many ways. We came back to this so many times that we #adapted and started #avoiding #talking too much, or talking about #important stuff, or #stupid stuff. We just cut too much stuff in the #past. We sinked each one in it's on way.
