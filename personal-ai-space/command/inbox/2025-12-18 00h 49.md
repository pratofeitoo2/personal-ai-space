## 📝 Notes & Thoughts

Yesterday around 11pm i arrived home after going to Brasília for picking up the kids and bring them to Zuco's first birthday party here at home in RJ. And until i succeed on this mission, a lot happened, and i mean really a lot. 

So, i'll try to recap as much as i can to fill the missing daily notes of recent past days. 

We have been planning to pick the kids up and bring them here because we wanted all siblings together on Zuco's first birthday, which is actually today Dec 18, and this plan resulted on plane tickets we bought for me getting there and back with the kids on the same day. But when the day came, my alarm got silent and i don't wake up in time for the flight. 

This made me a mix of angry, sorrow and sadness. Every thought and feeling i had ended up in guilt. 

So i  decided i would go driving for 24hrs until their home and bring them here no matter what. 

Terrible idea i must say. 

I got lost so many times in the middle of nowhere, no internet service available and with every road getting worst by the turn. I realized i was on danger traveling that way and decided head back home and recalculate my options before i got myself in trouble. 


I head back home with even more guilt and frustration. I felt I should give up and face the consequences of my failures. 

The price of traveling by bus was too high no matter the option available, which made me give up for real. 

I’ve had cost too much money with my mistakes already. Didn’t wanted to cost a single penny more. 

But then [[Tamara]] just bought the tickets even after I said I didn’t wanted it. 

I would leave the next day to get the kids. 

But to get at the bus station I needed to drive for about 3 hours. And so I did. 

Until the time my car gives me some alerts: breaks aren’t working correctly, stabilizing system and some other issues. I tried to leave the highway immediately and almost got the car into a wall because my breaks weren’t functioning properly. 

I couldn’t keep driving and risk getting myself in trouble. And once again I would loose the bus. 

Since I couldn’t leave the car by the road I drive home through a safer route. But almost got myself into a few accidents on the way. 

I got back home once again, my body shaking, my head aching and my emotions totally out of place. 

I felt I was under some curse or some magickal attack. And I didn’t get any fractal of energy or power to fight back. 

Whatever it was, it knocked me out. 

I had plenty of reasons to prove my point that the best choice was giving up. I kept costing more and more by every attempt I made. 



Here the story should be branched in two: the fight I had with Tamara [[2025-12-13]] and how this story moved on. 





So after the fighting, the crying and the awkward healing we had, we decided that I should give it another go instead of giving up. 

On the next day I would get a bus to the bus station and take another bus to Brasília. That was the plan.

The bus i was supposed to catch never showed up and i ended up taking a uber as expending more R$350 but i did arrived in time, went to DF, picked up the kids and got back home. 

And today is the birthday of [[PERSONAL/People/Luan|Luan]] and im feeling so messed up, so torn apart. I got myself isolated again, but i won't still like this much longer. I just cant. 

I am happy they're all here together, but i'm also feeling trapped insight my thoughts. Like i couldn't process what i've felt last week at all. 

I dont want to. Not in the slightest.
