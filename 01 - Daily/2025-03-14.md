I’m almost entirely convinced that my #marriage reached the end of the road. 

Not mainly for some big fight or problem, but for two people seeing and living two different worlds. 

I’m not afraid of being the entitled or egocentric on this story. I’m totally convinced I’m right. 

The list of reasons is large. And I can’t live pretending I don’t see all of her mechanisms, her belief system, her never ending scripted #behaviors. 

I can’t act like an idiot anymore. 

I’m incapable of being the support of her #immaturity, of being stuck her incapacity to live as an adult woman. 

It has become impossible to trade my #pain for her #childish needs. 

I might be a jerk for looking this way to a #marriage but is how I feel after all that happened. 

Is not lacking of #love. But I love a 30 years old child. 

I’ve lost so many #opportunities, #progress and #growth because of it. And when I lost I do not pay the price alone. 

It’s been five years of this. Five years of me paying the price while she’s learning about decency. It’s not fair. 

We are on thin ice. More than ever.
