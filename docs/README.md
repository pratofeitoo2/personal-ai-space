# Personal AI Space Architecture

Your personal agentic system for managing life, work, and growth.

## System Overview

This is **not** a showcase project—it's your operating system for living.

### Folders

- **engine/**: The AI brain. Agents, memory, databases, configs, logs.
- **vault/**: Your secrets (git-ignored). Credentials, passwords, API keys.
- **self/**: Your digital twin. Profile, habits, traits, needs, behaviors, insights.
- **command/**: Your control center. Tasks, calendar, finances, activities.
- **knowledge/**: Your library. Articles, notes, references, projects + curation index.
- **docs/**: This documentation.

## Getting Started

1. Update `self/profile.json` with your real data
2. Connect vault to your credential storage
3. Point engine config to your database locations
4. Begin populating command/ with real tasks and events

## Guiding Principles

- **Functional over fancy**: Use what works, not what looks good
- **Your data, your rules**: Nothing leaves without permission
- **Continuous learning**: System improves as it learns about you
- **Zero friction entry**: Capture everything, organize systematically
